I didn't plan this career. I survived it — one chapter at a time.

Paper route. Gas station. Dog food plant. Kansas wheat fields. Then I raised my hand for something most people wouldn't — I became a Navy rescue swimmer. The guy who jumps out of helicopters into the open ocean when someone's life depends on it. No committee. No second opinion. Just me, the water, and the job.

After that: chasing Russian submarines in the Mediterranean. Briefing the Secretary of the Navy during Desert Storm. The North Pole. Top Gun. A master's in applied physics. And then geophysicist aboard USS John F. Kennedy — one of five officers trusted to supervise operations for eighty aircraft and 5,500 crew. Five people. Eighty aircraft. Fifty-five hundred lives. That's not a job description. That's a pressure test with no practice round.

Left the Navy — walked straight into Wall Street. Built one of the first algorithmic equity trading desks. Ran global quant teams. President Obama appointed me to the SIPC board — Senate confirmed — where I helped oversee the $3.5 billion fund protecting American investors through Madoff and Lehman. Led Agile transformation across Wells Fargo, influencing thousands of engineering teams. Built healthcare startups with Johns Hopkins.

Today my wife Dr. Leigh Vinocur and I run two men's health clinics in Maryland. And I'm building Speaker Agent AI.

Every chapter taught me something the next one needed. AI just floored my accelerator.

Here's what started all of this.

My wife Leigh is a board-certified emergency physician, a national medical expert, a Fast Company contributor. She has credentials that most conference organizers would kill to have on their stage. But she was spending 40 hours a month doing what every expert does — cold emails that go nowhere, spreadsheets full of dead ends, researching events that weren't the right fit anyway.

So I built something to fix it. And it worked.

Speaker Agent AI is an AI-powered talent office that reads your actual work — your books, your articles, your talks — understands your specific expertise, and matches you to opportunities that fit your credentials, your voice, and your fees. Then it handles the outreach. In your voice. Not a template. Not a cold email blast.

No bureau taking 30%. No marketplace burying your profile next to 10,000 others. No middleman between you and the stage you've earned.
