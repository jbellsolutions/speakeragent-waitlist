Every Chapter Built the Next.

Tony D'Agostino
Founder, Speaker Agent AI · Navy Veteran · Operator

I didn't plan this career. I survived it — one chapter at a time.

Paper route. Gas station. Dog food plant. Kansas wheat fields. Then I raised my hand for something most people wouldn't — I became a Navy rescue swimmer. The guy who jumps out of helicopters into the open ocean when someone's life depends on it. No committee. No second opinion. Just me, the water, and the job.

After that: chasing Russian submarines in the Mediterranean. Briefing the Secretary of the Navy during Desert Storm. The North Pole. Top Gun. A master's in applied physics. Geophysicist aboard USS John F. Kennedy — one of five officers supervising operations for eighty aircraft and 5,500 crew. Five people. Eighty aircraft. Fifty-five hundred lives. That's not a job description. That's a pressure test with no practice round.

Left the Navy — walked straight into Wall Street. Built one of the first algorithmic equity trading desks at Wachovia. Global quant leadership at UBS. President Obama appointed me to the SIPC board — Senate confirmed — overseeing the $3.5 billion fund protecting American investors through Madoff and Lehman. Led Agile transformation across Wells Fargo, influencing thousands of engineering teams.

Today my wife Dr. Leigh Vinocur and I run two men's health clinics in Maryland. And I'm building Speaker Agent AI.

AI just floored my accelerator.
