The Big Idea

What If You Had a Talent Office That Never Slept?

Imagine an AI talent office that works around the clock. It reads your books. It studies your articles and past talks. It understands what you're actually an expert in, not just the keywords on your LinkedIn profile. Then it goes to work: finding speaking opportunities that match your credentials, your fee expectations, and your preferred markets. And when it reaches out on your behalf, it sounds like you wrote it. Because it learned your voice from your actual work.

This isn't a marketplace where you're one of 10,000 profiles waiting to be discovered. It's not a bureau that takes 30% and only calls when they need to fill a slot. And it's not a database of conference listings you have to sift through yourself.

This is a system built for one person: you. It knows your work. It protects your brand. And it does the 40 hours of monthly research, targeting, and outreach that you shouldn't be doing yourself.

The Real Problem

The Speaking Industry Has a Structural Problem.

The current model was built for event planners, not for speakers. Here's what that means for you.

40+ hours a month on admin. Researching events. Finding contacts. Writing pitches. Following up. Updating spreadsheets. Tracking who responded and who ghosted. This is the hidden cost of a speaking career that nobody warns you about. You didn't earn your expertise to spend half your week on business development.

Bureaus work for buyers, not speakers. The bureau model is built to serve event planners. They maintain a roster and wait for inbound requests. Unless you're already famous enough that planners ask for you by name, you're a line item in a catalog. And they take 25-30% for the privilege.

Marketplaces commoditize your expertise. When a board-certified physician sits next to a life coach with a podcast on the same platform, both reduced to a headshot and a tagline, something has gone wrong. Your credentials deserve more than a profile page and a prayer.

How It Works

The Four-Pillar Manifest Architecture

This is the engine behind Speaker Agent AI. Four systems working together to do what no bureau, marketplace, or assistant can.

Pillar 1

Core IP Ingestion

It reads your actual work. Your books, articles, published research, past talk descriptions, and media appearances. Not a keyword list. Not a bio you wrote in 2019. Your real intellectual property, ingested and understood at depth.

Pillar 2

Credential-First Positioning

Every outreach positions you as a peer, not a solicitor. Your board certifications, your publications, your institutional affiliations lead the conversation. You're presented as what you are: an authority reaching out to a relevant audience.

Pillar 3

Strategic Targeting

Your parameters. Your fee floors. Your preferred markets and industries. Your geographic constraints. The system targets opportunities that actually fit, not a spray-and-pray list of every conference with an open slot.

Pillar 4

Authentic Voice Matching

Every pitch, every outreach email, every follow-up sounds like you wrote it. Because the system learned your voice from your actual writing. No templates. No generic sales language. Your authentic voice, at scale.
